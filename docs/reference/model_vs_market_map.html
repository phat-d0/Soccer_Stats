Model vs Market Map

Where our match model disagreed with Pinnacle's price, and who turned out to be right. Every home, draw and away outcome, grouped by what the market said and how far the model differed.

The map

Each point is a group of outcomes. Across: the market's chance. Depth: how far the model's chance was above or below it. Height: how much more (or less) often the outcome happened than the market said. Blue means the model's disagreement was right, red means the market was closer, grey means the two agreed within 2 points. Faint points have under 200 outcomes. A ringed point's range stays clear of zero.
